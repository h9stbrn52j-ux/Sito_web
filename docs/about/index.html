At the European Institute of Design (IED), I developed a definitive visual language alongside strong project management skills, bridging the gap between cinema theory and modern aesthetics. 

I am equipped to oversee a project’s entire lifecycle — from the initial creative direction and scriptwriting, all the way to the final execution.

My portfolio is built on versatility and impact. 

I work as a Director across advertising, social media campaigns, and documentaries. 

In the cinematic field, I shape the visual narrative as a Director of Photography, while also bringing my technical expertise to other film projects as a Camera Operator.

My vision is backed by top-tier industry exposure. Embedded within the Olympic Broadcasting Services for the Milano-Cortina 2026 Winter Olympic and Paralympic Games, I gained inside access to elite live-production workflows and broadcast camera systems, absorbing the mechanics of the highest-pressure environment in the industry.

From polished commercials to gritty documentaries, I bring a tailored, cinematic approach to every frame.

Quality is the only metric.
